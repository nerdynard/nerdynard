Chess, and Go are considered as easy challenges since their rules are simple, tnumber of positions are finite.
However, they are infeasible due to the number of scenarios, albeit known, uncountable.
Take chess as an example, there are billion of board scenarios.
To "solve" chess, we would need a computer with 76Tb of RAM to remember the solutions.
Nonetheless, we would consider that Chess is solved, just not feasible.

The AI-based approach that AlphaZero follows estimate the number of winning scenarios from the current board.

This means that we can definitely say that AI is NOT memorization!
