Why control via buttons when speaking is way more natural.

<!--more-->

I have been playing video games such as X4 Foundation, and Satisfactory.
These games, among many others, are controlled in a first or third person perspective.
That is, you have to use WASD keys for directional movement, and mouse for steering the character's perspective.
Not to mention the jumping (space bar), crounching (Ctrl), running (Shift), action (left-click), and option (right-click).
This means that my hands are quite occupy for various actions already.

Pile on this, in X4 Foundation, the player can be the caption of an armada with ships in various roles. Changing their roles would need to go through a intricate sub-menu of sub-menus.
Similarly, in Satisfactory, the playe needs to choose the correct blueprint that is barried in complicated menu.
My plan to circumvent this hassle is to select the much needed blueprint's names or command the ship via voice.
